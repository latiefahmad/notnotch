This release makes NotNotch lighter on memory. Measured the honest way, from a warm session on this machine: the webview side of a closed notch sat at about 150 MB, most of it Chromium's own browser, GPU and utility processes plus one renderer holding every tool whether you opened it or not. Version 1.1.0 only pays for what you look at.

## What changed

- **Tools load on first use.** The app used to parse all 25 tools in one 1.05 MB script at startup; the tool that opens now loads as it opens, and the startup bundle is 490 kB.
- **The closed notch is quiet.** The small equalizer bars in the closed notch still follow the music, but the audio engine ticks 8 times a second for them instead of 40, so a closed notch moves a fraction of the data it did — and the moment a tool opens, its equalizer runs at the full 40 again.
- **Clipboard cards fetch their thumbnails.** A copy used to send every card's base64 image in every history update; now a card pulls its thumbnail when you look at the clipboard. Closed things keep no image data, and history updates are plain metadata.
- **Lower memory target for every window.** When the notch has been closed for a while, WebView2's memory target goes down for the notch, the mirrors and the timer together, not just the one window that asked.
- **Terminal scrollback:** 1000 lines by default (was 5000), matching VS Code's default; a day of output no longer holds the whole app in memory.

## Save memory (Windows)

Settings → General has a new switch: every window runs in **one process** instead of the browser, GPU, utility and renderer processes WebView2 starts on its own. Measured, the whole app then sits at about a third of the RAM. The price is the renderer sandbox: a plugin page would run inside the app, so this is a choice, not the default, and it applies at the next start.

## Under the hood

- `audio_rate` lets the frontend choose the FFT tick (fine/coarse) per equalizer; `clipboard_thumbnail` makes card previews on demand with a small LRU cache; `memory_low` reaches every webview.
- Shelf state moved out of its view, so Capture and Downloads can add drops without loading the tool.
